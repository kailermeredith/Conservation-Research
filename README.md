# Conservation-Research
This repository houses independent research projects examining conservation science, ecology, biodiversity, and environmental governance.

Research emphasizes evidence synthesis, scientific communication, and the intersection of ecological science with public policy.
## Current Research Areas

- Citizen science
- Species distribution modeling
- Conservation ecology
- Climate governance
- Environmental ethics

## Repository Contents

- Research manuscripts
- Literature reviews
- Figures and graphics
- Supporting datasets
- References

Additional manuscripts and research projects will be added over time.
